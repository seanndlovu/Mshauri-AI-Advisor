gri Challenge (instead of Agri Quiz)

A gamified learning platform where farmers, students, extension officers, agribusinesses, and agriculture enthusiasts compete with each other while learning.

Core Objectives
Drive daily visits to Maricho Media.
Increase time spent on the platform.
Encourage users to return every day.
Build an agriculture community.
Reward learning rather than guessing.
Collect useful engagement data.
Gameplay
Daily Challenge
5–10 new agriculture questions every day.
Questions sourced from:
Maricho Media articles
videos
podcasts
AGRITEX knowledge
seasonal farming information
local Zimbabwean farming practices

Users have 24 hours to complete the challenge.

Instead of immediately answering questions, users can:

📖 Read today's Maricho Media article

🎧 Listen to a podcast

▶️ Watch a short video

Then play the quiz.

This drives traffic directly to Maricho Media.

Should it be Global or Maricho Community?

I recommend starting with a Maricho Community rather than a fully global platform.

Phase 1: Maricho Community (Recommended)

Everyone who signs up becomes part of the Maricho learning community.

Benefits:

Drives traffic to Maricho Media.
Strengthens the Maricho brand.
Builds loyalty.
Easier to moderate.
Encourages repeat visits.
Supports your mission of agricultural education.

Users still compete with all registered Maricho users, making the leaderboards active and engaging.

Phase 2: Global Expansion

Once the platform has traction, you can expand to:

Zimbabwe
SADC
Africa
Global users

Add regional leaderboards and localized content while keeping Maricho Media as the central hub.

Additional Features to Differentiate the Platform
Crop-specific learning paths (maize, tomatoes, poultry, cattle, goats, etc.)
Weather-based quizzes tailored to the user's location and season.
Photo challenges (identify pests, diseases, weeds, or nutrient deficiencies from images).
Weekly AI Coach that highlights strengths, weaknesses, and recommended Maricho articles.
Achievement badges for milestones such as "Soil Health Expert," "Livestock Champion," or "Climate Smart Farmer."
Referral rewards for inviting new users to the Maricho community.
Final Recommendation

Don't build "Agri Quiz." Build an Agri Learning Ecosystem centered on Maricho Media.

The quiz should be the engagement engine—not the product itself. Every question should encourage users to read articles, watch videos, listen to podcasts, and participate in the Maricho community. This creates a sustainable flywheel:

Discover content → Learn → Play → Earn rewards → Climb leaderboards → Share → Return daily → Consume more Maricho content.

This approach aligns much better with your long-term goal of increasing Maricho Media's traffic, engagement, and impact while creating a vibrant agricultural community.
