# Maricho Media Community Rules

**Effective Date:** June 22, 2026

These Community Rules apply to all users of:

* Mshauri AI
* mshauri.marichomedia.com
* Maricho Media websites
* WhatsApp services and chatbots
* Mobile applications
* Social media communities
* User forums and discussion spaces

Our goal is to create a safe, respectful, informative, and productive environment for farmers, entrepreneurs, students, researchers, journalists, and community members across Zimbabwe, SADC, and Africa.

Failure to follow these Rules may result in content removal, account restrictions, suspension, termination, or reporting to relevant authorities.

## Rule 1: Respect Other People

Treat all users with dignity and respect.

Do not:

* Harass, bully, threaten, or intimidate others;
* Encourage violence against individuals or groups;
* Target people based on race, nationality, ethnicity, disability, age, religion, gender, sexual orientation, or other protected characteristics;
* Use hate speech or discriminatory language;
* Engage in personal attacks or abusive behavior.

Healthy debate is encouraged. Abuse is not.

## Rule 2: No Harmful or Dangerous Content

Do not post or share content that:

* Promotes violence;
* Encourages self-harm;
* Encourages animal cruelty;
* Promotes terrorism or extremist activity;
* Provides instructions for illegal or dangerous activities;
* Encourages harmful agricultural, veterinary, medical, or chemical practices.

Content that could endanger people, animals, crops, or property may be removed without notice.

## Rule 3: No Fraud, Scams, or Misrepresentation

Do not:

* Impersonate individuals, organizations, businesses, government agencies, or experts;
* Misrepresent qualifications or professional credentials;
* Operate scams, pyramid schemes, or fraudulent programs;
* Share misleading financial, agricultural, veterinary, or business information intended to deceive others.

Users must act honestly and in good faith.

## Rule 4: Respect Privacy

Do not publish or share:

* Personal phone numbers without consent;
* Home addresses;
* Identity documents;
* Banking details;
* Passwords or login credentials;
* Private communications without authorization.

Respect the privacy and safety of others at all times.

## Rule 5: Use Accurate Information

Users should make reasonable efforts to provide accurate information.

Do not knowingly:

* Spread misinformation;
* Share false agricultural advice;
* Fabricate research findings;
* Publish manipulated information intended to mislead others.

Mshauri AI may generate incorrect information. Users should independently verify important information before acting upon it.

## Rule 6: No Illegal Activities

Do not use the Services to:

* Violate any applicable law;
* Sell illegal goods or services;
* Commit fraud;
* Launder money;
* Facilitate cybercrime;
* Distribute malware;
* Engage in unauthorized access to systems or data.

Illegal activity may be reported to law enforcement authorities.

## Rule 7: Respect Intellectual Property

Only upload content that you own or have permission to use.

Do not:

* Upload copyrighted material without authorization;
* Infringe trademarks;
* Share pirated software;
* Copy proprietary content without permission.

Users remain responsible for all content they submit.

## Rule 8: No Spam or Platform Abuse

Do not:

* Send spam messages;
* Flood discussions;
* Manipulate engagement metrics;
* Create fake accounts;
* Use automated bots without authorization;
* Scrape platform content without permission.

We reserve the right to remove spam and abusive activity.

## Rule 9: Responsible Use of AI

When using Mshauri AI:

* Verify important recommendations before implementation;
* Do not present AI-generated content as professional advice without verification;
* Do not use AI to create harmful, deceptive, or fraudulent content;
* Do not use AI outputs to impersonate others.

Users remain responsible for decisions made using AI-generated information.

## Rule 10: Agricultural and Livestock Safety

Agricultural and livestock recommendations should be used responsibly.

Do not:

* Encourage unsafe pesticide use;
* Promote harmful veterinary treatments;
* Recommend illegal chemicals or substances;
* Share advice likely to cause harm to crops, livestock, wildlife, or people.

Always consult qualified professionals where appropriate.

## Rule 11: Child Safety

We have zero tolerance for content that:

* Exploits children;
* Endangers children;
* Sexualizes minors;
* Encourages abuse of children.

Any such content will be removed immediately and may be reported to relevant authorities.

## Rule 12: Graphic and Sensitive Content

Users must not post excessively graphic, disturbing, or violent content.

Where educational or newsworthy content contains sensitive material, it should be appropriately labeled and shared responsibly.

## Rule 13: Reporting Violations

Users are encouraged to report violations of these Rules.

Maricho Media may investigate reports and take appropriate action, including:

* Content removal;
* Warning notices;
* Account restrictions;
* Temporary suspensions;
* Permanent bans;
* Referral to authorities where required.

## Rule 14: Enforcement

Maricho Media reserves the right to:

* Remove content;
* Restrict visibility of content;
* Suspend accounts;
* Terminate accounts;
* Limit access to services;

where it reasonably believes a violation of these Rules, applicable law, or platform policies has occurred.

## Rule 15: Community First

Mshauri AI and Maricho Media exist to support learning, collaboration, innovation, agriculture, journalism, entrepreneurship, and community development.

Users should contribute constructively and help create a trustworthy, respectful, and useful environment for everyone.
