---
name: Advertiser analytics privacy
description: Privacy boundary for advert measurement and sponsor-facing reporting
---

Advertiser reporting must use consented, aggregated advert events only; never expose names, emails, phone numbers, account IDs, conversation IDs, message content, raw IPs, or visitor tokens.

**Why:** Mshauri’s analytics promise is privacy-based, and sponsor reports need credible audience numbers without turning advertising measurement into user surveillance.

**How to apply:** Keep advert impressions, clicks, reach, and CTR behind Owner access, label figures as measured/consented where appropriate, and preserve the separation between web analytics and operational WhatsApp data.
